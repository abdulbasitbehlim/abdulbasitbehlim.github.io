01 · The researcher

Biologist in thinking.
Builder in practice.

My work sits where biotechnology meets computational biology.

I am pursuing an MSc in Biotechnology with a specialization in Industrial Biotechnology at Gujarat Biotechnology University. I’m interested in turning complex biological questions into transparent computational workflows that researchers can inspect, validate and improve.

My current work connects biological sequence analysis, CRISPR design, bioprocess data, machine learning and phage research. Across these areas, I care about the same things: clear evidence, useful tools and reproducible science.

“I want to build computational biology systems that are scientifically useful, transparent and reproducible.”
