Experience

Research & academic experience

Research Project – Computational Drug Design

Indian Institute of Technology, Roorkee (IIT Roorkee)

June 2026 · One-month intensive research project

Project Title: Structure-based in silico study of human beta-cardiac myosin inhibitors associated with cardiomyopathies

Worked on identifying potential inhibitors for human β-cardiac myosin to address cardiomyopathies using computer-aided drug design (CADD). Key activities included:

  • Protein structure visualization & preparation using UCSF ChimeraX and Schrödinger suite
  • Virtual library screening of FDA-approved drugs
  • Molecular docking with HADDOCK and Schrödinger Glide
  • Validation of top protein–ligand complexes via Molecular Dynamics (MD) simulations in Schrödinger

Gained end-to-end hands-on experience in structural bioinformatics and computational drug discovery workflows.
